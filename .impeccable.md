## Design Context

### Users

Industrial robot integrators and factory safety engineers use this application at engineering workstations to describe a robot cell, maintain safety zones, import offline motion trajectories, inspect envelope intersections, and review interlock evidence before separate certified acceptance activities.

### Brand Personality

Precise, restrained, and safety-first. The interface should support calm technical judgment and make uncertainty, evidence, and responsibility boundaries explicit.

### Aesthetic Direction

A light industrial control-console aesthetic with charcoal structure, off-white work surfaces, safety yellow for attention, and restrained green/red state accents. It must feel like a durable engineering instrument rather than a marketing site. Avoid dark neon styling, decorative gradients, oversized hero copy, floating card grids, and consumer-app playfulness.

### Design Principles

1. Keep high-frequency actions and evidence dense, aligned, and scannable.
2. Pair every status color with a label and icon or shape so meaning never depends on color alone.
3. Give geometry, trajectory, and findings visual priority while keeping controls predictable.
4. Separate simulation evidence from acceptance authority in both copy and interaction hierarchy.
5. Preserve full functionality on narrow screens through reflow and deliberate horizontal scrolling, never by hiding critical actions.
